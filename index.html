Yoot Rules:

Set Up: Each player or team starts with their pieces off of the board.

On your turn, roll the sticks to determine how many spaces to move your piece forward. There are 6 possible rolls, with the X side representing the round side of the stick:

The 6th possible roll, called back do, is when only the first stick, represented by a black outline, is the only stick flat side up. When a back do is rolled, your piece moves backwards 1 space. If you do not have a piece on the board and roll a back do, you may not place your piece on the board.

Move your piece forward beginning from start (the green square). If the opposing player or team's piece is already occupying the space, their piece is bumped off the board and back to start!

If you roll a mo or yut, take another turn!

The first player or team to move their piece all the way around board, including moving their piece past the final square (the red square), wins!
